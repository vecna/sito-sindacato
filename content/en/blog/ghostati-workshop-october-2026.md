<section>
<h2>How a session runs</h2>

<div class="content-page__step">1. Bring water-based face paint and extra brushes.
Start with the darkest and lightest colours.</div>
<figure class="content-page__figure">
<img src="/images/ghostati/workshops/tools1-makeup.jpg" alt="A children's face-paint kit with brushes" loading="lazy">
</figure>

<div class="content-page__step">2. Open the <a href="/ghostati/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">lab</a>
and save your face while it is still clean. That gives you an ID
to test against.</div>
<figure class="content-page__figure">
<img src="/images/ghostati/workshops/ws-lab-save-id.jpg" alt="The lab with a face detected, landmarks drawn, and the readout showing match 0.00 against a 0.58 threshold, recognised as ID zero" loading="lazy">
<figcaption class="content-page__caption">Saved. The readout now
reads your distance from that baseline, against the 0.58
threshold. At rest it sits near zero.</figcaption>
</figure>

<div class="content-page__step">3. Paint. Break the lines of the
face. In workshops people usually pair up and paint each
other.</div>
<figure class="content-page__figure">
<img src="/images/ghostati/workshops/ws1-painting-1.jpg" alt="Two participants painting each other's faces" loading="lazy">
</figure>

<div class="content-page__step">4. Go back to the webcam. Success is
either no face detected at all, or your ID marked
"eluded".</div>
<figure class="content-page__figure">
<img src="/images/ghostati/workshops/ws1-checking-1.jpg" alt="The lab losing the detection box off the painted face" loading="lazy">
<figcaption class="content-page__caption">The detection square is
no longer on the face. Screen from the first version of the
lab.</figcaption>
</figure>

<div class="content-page__step">5. Second check, no lab needed: any
phone app with a face filter. If it asks you to find a
face, you did it.</div>
<figure class="content-page__figure">
<img src="/images/ghostati/workshops/ws1-checking-2.jpg" alt="A phone camera app failing to find the painted face" loading="lazy">
<figcaption class="content-page__caption">The phone does not
unlock either.</figcaption>
</figure>

<div class="content-page__step">6. Reduce the paint and retest.</div>
<figure class="content-page__figure">
<img src="/images/ghostati/workshops/ws2-facepainted.jpg" alt="A heavily painted face" loading="lazy">
<figcaption class="content-page__caption">The developer, painted
by a 12-year-old kid. The camouflage worked... but would you go
out like this?</figcaption>
</figure>

<div class="content-page__step">7. Share what worked. Contributions
are ranked by how subtle they are, so a look that takes two lines
is worth more here than one that takes a mask.</div>
<figure class="content-page__figure">
<img src="/images/ghostati/workshops/ws-lab-upload-consent.jpg" alt="The lab upload screen: a recorded clip with its metadata, a note field for moderators, an unticked consent checkbox and a disabled submit button" loading="lazy">
<figcaption class="content-page__caption">Record a clip, add what
you did, then consent. Nothing leaves the browser until that
box is ticked, and the receipt code you get back is what
deletes it later.</figcaption>
</figure>
</section>
