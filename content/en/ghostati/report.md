<section class="content-section" id="before-you-write">
<h2>Before you write anything.</h2>
<p>
If you want to report, remember that browsers might save and
record digital activities; it happens
automatically and without explicit knowledge.
Consider these hints before preparing a report.
</p>
<ul class="opsec-list">
<li>Do not tell people who might disclose your reporting plans.</li>
<li>Do not use your employer's device, account or network to
read this page or to prepare
anything.</li>
<li>Report what you already know and can access within your normal permissions.
Do not seek additional material for us.</li>
<li>Photos and documents carry hidden information: timestamps,
device IDs, GPS coordinates, printer
tracking dots. We do not publish material we receive; we use
it as a lead. When in doubt,
describe what you saw in your own words instead of uploading
the original file.</li>
<li>When you are ready, use <a href="https://www.torproject.org/download/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Tor Browser</a> to open the
submission page, from a device and a
network that are not tied to your workplace.</li>
</ul>
<div class="callout">
<p>
These practices reduce avoidable exposure; no reporting
channel removes every risk. Choose the
precautions that match your situation, and do not continue if
doing so would put you in danger.
If you need help assessing that risk, a digital-rights or
whistleblower-support organisation may
be able to help before you send anything.
</p>
</div>

platform is based on <a href="https://www.globaleaks.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GlobaLeaks</a>, the

is reachable over Tor.
</p>
<p>
Each questionnaire asks only what a person in that position can
answer, and almost every field is optional, answer only what you

<section class="content-section" id="what-we-do">
<h2>What happens to what you send.</h2>
<p>
The submission screen names the editorial and research team members
who will read your report. These rules apply to every channel:
</p>
<ul class="opsec-list">
<li><strong>Nothing you send is published as received.</strong>
Reports are leads, not articles. A
document or a screenshot is used to verify and to direct the
next test, and it stays inside the
reporting system.</li>
<li><strong>Everything is corroborated before it is
used.</strong> Each questionnaire ends by asking
how you know and what would confirm it. A single-source claim
is treated as a question to
investigate, not as a finding to repeat.</li>
<li><strong>We follow your restrictions.</strong> At the end of
each form you can tell us to use a report only as a lead, not
to quote you, not to name a place
or an organisation, or not to contact anyone before asking
you. We honour that.</li>
<li><strong>What returns to the public is minimised.</strong> A
finding becomes public only after
editorial and security review, stripped of anything that
points to a source: aggregated where
several reports agree, redacted where one does not. Verified
findings can change the
<a href="https://ghostmaxxing.vecna.eu/references/">reference archive</a>, the tests run in
the
<a href="/ghostati/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">lab</a> and what is published
<a href="https://ghostmaxxing.vecna.eu/fediverse.html">in the Fediverse</a>. Confidential
reports never go there directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>
<br>
We may suggest a newsroom or advocacy organisation through your receipt code.
We will not forward anything without your agreement.
</p>
<p>
Reports that receive no further activity are deleted from the
reporting system after 180 days. A
case that is still open can be kept longer by the people
handling it, and you can ask, through the
receipt, for a report to be deleted at any time.
</p>

<h3>What we cannot promise.</h3>
<ul class="opsec-list">
<li><strong>Legal protection.</strong> Whistleblower laws,
including the EU directive and its
national versions, mostly protect reports made to your
employer, to a designated authority or,
under conditions, to the public. Reporting to a research
project is usually not one of the
protected routes. If your position depends on legal
protection, talk to a whistleblower-support
organisation or a lawyer first; the resources above can point
you to one.</li>
<li><strong>Intervention in your case.</strong> We investigate
systems. We are not able to appeal a
decision, represent you or act on your behalf, though we can
suggest who might.</li>
<li><strong>A reply on a deadline.</strong> A person reads new
reports at least weekly and answers
through the receipt conversation. Nothing is forgotten, but
this is volunteer work.</li>
<li><strong>Payment.</strong> We do not pay for information, and
we would treat an offer to sell it
as a reason for caution.</li>
</ul>
</section>

<section class="content-section" id="how-it-works">
<h2>How the submission works.</h2>
<p>
The reporting node is a separate GlobaLeaks installation with
two addresses. The first is a normal
HTTPS site. The second is an Onion Service, opened with Tor
Browser, which hides from your network
and from us where you are connecting from, and resists selective
blocking. Prefer the Onion address
whenever you can.
</p>
<div class="receipt-callout">
<span class="receipt-callout__code" aria-hidden="true">.onion</span>
<p>
HTTPS: <a href="https://raccontaci.nina.watch" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">raccontaci.nina.watch</a><br>
Tor: <a href="http://g3p4uwkfsbrnx2ydlriz7rq7kh6h4jowmkrfbq7xpvhltbarf5qumgyd.onion/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><code>g3p4uwkfsbrnx2ydlriz7rq7kh6h4jowmkrfbq7xpvhltbarf5qumgyd.onion</code></a>
</p>
</div>
<ul class="opsec-list">
<li>You do not create an account, and nothing in the forms asks
for your name or contact details.</li>
<li>Most fields are optional. Answer what you can and skip the
rest.</li>
<li>Write in the language you think in. We read Italian and
English directly and translate the
rest.</li>
<li>Files are optional everywhere. A description in your own
words is often safer and just as
useful.</li>
<li>If Whistleblowing is a new concept for you, check out <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whistleblower" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">this Wikipedia
article</a>.</li>
</ul>

<section class="content-section" id="after-you-submit">
<h2>Submitting is the beginning, not the end.</h2>
<p>
At the end of the form you are given a private <strong>receipt
code</strong>.
</p>
<div class="receipt-callout">
<span class="receipt-callout__code" aria-hidden="true">•••• ••••
•••• ••••</span>
<p>
That code is the only way, for you or for us, to reopen your
report. Save it somewhere safe,
offline if you can. If it is lost, the conversation cannot be
recovered. That is by design.
</p>
</div>
<p>
Come back and use it. We may have follow-up questions, we may
want to check one detail before a
finding moves forward, and you may want to add something you
left out or withdraw something you
regret. A source who keeps that thread open helps an
investigation go much further than a single
anonymous drop. Secure reporting works when there is a network
of technical, legal and editorial
support around it rather than a box that swallows a report and
returns nothing, which is the same
thinking behind GlobaLeaks' own
<a href="https://globaleaks.org/2026/06/23/supporting-the-house-of-whistleblowers-expanding-the-ecosystem-around-secure-reporting/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">House of
Whistleblowers</a> initiative.
</p>
