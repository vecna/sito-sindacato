This is not a collection of “makeup patterns that always work”. It is a visual notebook for explaining *dazzling*, finding a place to start and showing how people work during a workshop.

When a card includes a video, the clip shows the check recorded in the lab. A photograph alone cannot tell us whether the detector lost the face or the descriptor crossed the threshold.
