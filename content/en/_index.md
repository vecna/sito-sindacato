# The Universal Digital Union

In the lexicon of the digital economy, one deceptive word dominates: _user_. The term user suggests passivity and consumption. But contemporary digital infrastructure depends on users as **producers of value**. Every search, click, scroll, and comment generates data that feeds machine learning systems and financial platforms, operating as a massive **diffuse factory**.

The Universal Digital Union serves as a transnational collective defense to redefine our relationship with these technological infrastructures and safeguard technological sovereignty.
