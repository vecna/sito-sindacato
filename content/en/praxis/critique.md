Critique means maintaining constant vigilance to expose technology’s opaque processes and make them visible. We need to translate and explain these dynamics to a wider public, breaking through the promises and lies of technocapitalist narratives and dismantling the myth of inevitable automation.

It also means **inviting and protecting inside sources through dedicated leaking and whistleblowing channels**. Among those working in the data industry are allies who experience this conflict as a daily compromise. They can help us understand parts of the market that remain invisible to people outside it.
