Boycott means refusing tools and platforms built on the exploitation of artificial intelligence. It means deserting closed systems and withdrawing our time, data, legitimacy and organisational dependence from them.

A boycott is not a test of individual purity. It works when it seeks out, builds and strengthens alternatives that return greater autonomy to people while delivering speed in practice and genuine energy savings.
