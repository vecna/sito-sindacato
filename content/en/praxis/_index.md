Two centuries ago, industrial workers could make their power felt most directly by stopping the production line through a strike. Under data capitalism — built on knowledge extraction and surveillance — we also need other tools.

These five forms of praxis are neither a rulebook nor a finished repertoire. They are directions for work that we can test, document and revise collectively.
