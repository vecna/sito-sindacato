Subversion means bending machines and infrastructure until they diverge from the producer’s original purpose. Interfaces can turn automation back against those who impose it and open possibilities that the initial design sought to exclude.

Subversion makes the centralisation and commodification of culture more expensive, inefficient and less attractive as a business. It does not celebrate the machine; it redistributes agency towards those who are normally expected only to submit to its rules.
