Obfuscation means actively disrupting the collection and predatory use of data by introducing noise, ambiguity and interference. Documenting these practices exposes the fragility of extractive models and degrades their technical reliability.

The purpose is not indiscriminate confusion. It is to neutralise the strategic advantage of large-scale data collectors and dismantle the claims of precision made by their marketing. Interference becomes praxis when other people can observe, repeat and understand it.
