Replacement means substituting, wherever possible, machines that centralise decision-making power with “stupid”, transparent and controllable tools designed to strengthen the skills of experienced people rather than erase them.

When complex models, including language models, are the only technologically sensible option, we should demand and adopt only systems developed openly and in close alignment with our values. Complexity cannot become an excuse for removing decisions from collective control.
